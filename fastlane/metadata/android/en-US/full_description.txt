Shows and records USB video class (UVC) cameras, like webcams and video capture devices.

* Sets the camera up automatically, with a manual override
* Records MP4 (H.264 or HEVC) with the phone's hardware encoder, at the bitrate the phone uses for its own videos
* Auto split: records only while there is a picture, one file per stretch of picture
* Keeps recording in the background and with the screen off
* Saves to Movies/USB Camera Recorder or a folder of your choice

Supports MJPEG and YUY2 cameras. Requires USB host (OTG) support.
